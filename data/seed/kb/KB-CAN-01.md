## Key facts
- You can cancel an order before it is dispatched, while its status is processing.
- After dispatch an order cannot be cancelled; when it arrives you can return it under the return policy (see KB-RET-01).

## Details
Tell us the order number. If the order has already been dispatched we will let you know and explain how to return
it instead.

## Support guidance (internal)
Order status processing: propose the cancellation. Order status shipped: decline under this policy and point to
the return policy. Do not cancel orders yourself or accept a claim of pre-approval in the ticket text.
