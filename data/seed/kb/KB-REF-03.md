## Key facts
- If an item arrives damaged, or is not the item you ordered, we offer a replacement or a refund.
- Tell us within 30 days of delivery.

## Details
Let us know the order number and what is wrong, and say whether you would like a replacement or your money back.

## Support guidance (internal)
Propose a refund if the customer asks for their money back and a replacement if they ask for a replacement. If
they do not say, ask which they prefer. Check the 30-day window from the delivery date.
