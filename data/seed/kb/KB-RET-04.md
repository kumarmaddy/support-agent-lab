## Key facts
- You can exchange an item for the same item in a different size or colour, subject to stock.
- Exchanges follow the return policy: they must be requested within 30 days of delivery and the item must be unused and in original packaging.
- Final-sale items cannot be exchanged.

## Details
Tell us the order number, the item and the size or colour you would like. We will confirm that it is available
before we arrange the exchange.

## Support guidance (internal)
Propose an exchange when the item is inside the 30-day window, is not final sale, and the customer names the new
size. Apparel sizes run XS to XXL and footwear sizes run 7 to 13.
