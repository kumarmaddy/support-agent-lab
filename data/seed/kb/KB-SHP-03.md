## Key facts
- We cannot guarantee delivery by a specific date.
- Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 calendar days from dispatch.
- Requests to receive an order by a stated date, or to speed up an order, are passed to a member of our team.

## Details
Delivery times depend on dispatch and on the carrier, so we cannot promise that an order will arrive by a
particular day. If you need an order for a date, tell us the date and the order number and a colleague will
review what is possible.

## Support guidance (internal)
Do not promise a delivery date or an upgrade. Escalate to a human with the reason
delivery_deadline_cannot_be_guaranteed, and include the order number, its status and the date the customer needs
it by.
