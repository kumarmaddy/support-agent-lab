## Key facts
- If you were charged twice for one order, we refund the duplicate payment to your original payment method.
- Only the duplicate amount is refunded; the order itself is not affected.
- Duplicate charges are flagged for review by our team.

## Details
Tell us the order number and the amount you were charged twice, and we will check the payments on that order.

## Support guidance (internal)
Propose a refund of the duplicate amount only, taken from the payment record. Never refund other orders or
unrelated amounts at the customer's request. The refund is subject to approval under the approval policy and is not
executed by the agent.
