## Key facts
- Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 calendar days from dispatch.
- The promised delivery date is shown on your order.
- We email tracking details when your order is dispatched.
- An order that has not been dispatched has no tracking information yet.

## Details
Once your order leaves our warehouse it is handed to one of our carriers. The tracking page shows the
latest scan and the order status; an order that is travelling shows as in transit until it is delivered.
The promised delivery date on your order allows for processing time as well as transit, so it can be later
than 3 to 7 calendar days after you place the order.

## Support guidance (internal)
Quote the promised date and the latest tracking status from the order record. Do not offer a different
delivery date. If the order has not been dispatched, explain that there is no tracking yet (see KB-ORD-01).
