## Key facts
- A new order shows as processing until it is dispatched.
- When an order is dispatched its status changes to shipped and we email tracking details.
- An order that is processing can still be cancelled or have its delivery address changed.
- The promised delivery date is shown on the order.

## Details
Processing means we have received your order and it has not yet left our warehouse. After dispatch the order shows as shipped, then delivered. See the articles on cancelling an
order and changing a delivery address for what can be done while an order is processing.

## Support guidance (internal)
If an order is processing, say it has not been dispatched yet and that there is no tracking. Quote the promised
date from the order record. Do not state how long processing will take.
