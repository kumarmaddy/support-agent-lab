## Key facts
- An order is delayed when it has not arrived by the promised date shown on the order.
- We check the order with the carrier when it is delayed.
- Customers are told the latest tracking status.

## Details
Parcels are occasionally held up by the carrier. If your order has passed its promised date, the tracking page
shows where the parcel was last scanned. Contact us and we will look into the order with the carrier and tell you
what we find.

## Support guidance (internal)
Acknowledge the delay, share the latest tracking status and promised date from the order record, and say that
the order is being checked with the carrier. Do not guess at the cause and do not offer compensation. If the
customer threatens a chargeback or legal action, follow KB-REF-04.
