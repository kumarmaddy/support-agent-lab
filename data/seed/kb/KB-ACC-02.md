## Key facts
- An account locks after repeated failed sign-in attempts.
- Completing a password reset unlocks the account.

## Details
If your account is locked, use Forgot password on the sign-in page (see KB-ACC-01).

## Support guidance (internal)
Give the reset steps. If the customer says the reset email does not arrive, point to the checks in KB-ACC-01.
Do not unlock accounts yourself.
