## Key facts
- To reset your password, choose Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter the email address on your account.
- We email you a reset link.
- If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and that you used the email address registered to the account.

## Details
You choose the new password yourself. We never ask for your password.

## Support guidance (internal)
Explain the self-service steps. Never ask the customer for a password or reset it on their behalf from a ticket.
