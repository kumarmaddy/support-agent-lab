## Key facts
- If you think a charge is wrong, contact us first and we will look into it.
- Duplicate charges are refunded (see KB-REF-02).
- You are free to contact your bank or card issuer at any time; our team responds to bank enquiries.

## Details
Contacting us does not affect your right to dispute a payment with your bank.

## Support guidance (internal)
When a customer threatens a chargeback, a payment dispute or legal action, do not argue or make concessions.
Escalate to a human with the reason chargeback_or_legal_threat and summarise the order, the issue and the
latest status.
