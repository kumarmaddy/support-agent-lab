## Key facts
- You can return an item within 30 days of delivery.
- The 30 days are calendar days and the day of delivery counts as day 0; day 30 is still inside the window and day 31 is not.
- Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
- Return labels are free.
- Final-sale items cannot be returned (see KB-RET-03).

## Details
Count from the delivery date shown on your order. A return requested on the thirtieth day after delivery is
accepted; one requested on the thirty-first day is not. Refunds follow once we receive the item (see KB-REF-01).

## Support guidance (internal)
Work out the number of days since delivery from the delivery date in the order record. Within 30 days: propose a
return label. 31 days or more: decline under this policy and say why. Check the item is not final sale first.
