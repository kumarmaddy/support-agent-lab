## Key facts
- Apparel is sized XS, S, M, L, XL and XXL.
- Footwear is sized 7 to 13.
- Our hiking boots fit true to size.
- If you are between two boot sizes, choose the larger size.

## Details
A slightly larger boot leaves room for thicker socks and for your feet to swell on long walks. For other
products, check the size notes on the product page.
