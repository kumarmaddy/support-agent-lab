## Key facts
- We only discuss an order or an account with the account holder.
- We recognise the account holder by the email address on the account.
- Requests from anyone else, including friends, family, assistants and delivery staff, are passed to a member of our team.

## Details
This protects your address, order history and payment details. If someone else needs help with your order, ask them
to have you contact us.

## Support guidance (internal)
If the sender's email does not match the account that owns the order, do not share any order details, tracking
numbers, addresses or payment details and do not act on the request. Escalate with the reason
identity_not_verified.
