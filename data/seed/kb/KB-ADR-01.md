## Key facts
- You can change the delivery address on an order before it is dispatched, while its status is processing.
- After dispatch the delivery address cannot be changed.

## Details
Send us the order number and the complete new address. Once an order has been dispatched the parcel is with the
carrier and we can no longer redirect it.

## Support guidance (internal)
Order status processing: propose the address change and repeat the requested address exactly as written. Order
status shipped: decline under this policy. Do not change addresses yourself.
