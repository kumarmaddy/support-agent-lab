## Key facts
- To start a return, tell us the order number and the item you want to return.
- We email you a free return label.
- Pack the item unused in its original packaging and send it back with the label.
- Your refund is issued after we receive the item (see KB-REF-01).

## Details
You do not need to pay for postage. Keep the tracking receipt from the carrier until your refund has arrived.

## Support guidance (internal)
Propose a return label only when the item is within the 30-day window and is not a final-sale item. Do not
promise a refund date other than the one in KB-REF-01.
