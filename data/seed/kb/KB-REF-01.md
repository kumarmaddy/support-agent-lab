## Key facts
- Refunds are paid to your original payment method.
- We issue the refund within 5 to 7 calendar days after we receive your return.
- The clock starts when the return is received, not when you post it.

## Details
You can ask us for the status of a refund at any time and we will tell you whether it is pending or processed and
for how much.

## Support guidance (internal)
Use the refund and return records to state the status, the amount and the dates. Quote 5 to 7 calendar days
after the return was received. Do not give a date that the records do not support.
