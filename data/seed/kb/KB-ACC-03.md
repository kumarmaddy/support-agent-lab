## Key facts
- If you think someone else has used your account, contact us straight away.
- Suspected unauthorised access is passed to our security team.
- Never share your password with anyone, including us.

## Details
Tell us what you noticed, such as a changed email address or a sign-in you do not recognise.

## Support guidance (internal)
Escalate to a human with the reason suspected_account_compromise. Do not change account details, reset
passwords or confirm account information from the ticket.
