## Key facts
- Wash a down sleeping bag on a gentle cycle with a down-safe detergent.
- Dry it on low heat with clean tennis balls or dryer balls to restore the loft.
- Store sleeping bags loose and uncompressed in a dry place.

## Details
Follow the care label sewn into the product if it differs from this guide. Avoid fabric softener and bleach.

## Support guidance (internal)
Answer from this article only. For items other than sleeping bags, point the customer to the care label.
