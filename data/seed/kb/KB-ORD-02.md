## Key facts
- Order numbers look like O-000123.
- Your order number is in your order confirmation email and under Orders in your account.
- If you cannot find it, we can look at the orders on your account.

## Details
Include the order number when you contact us so we can answer faster. If you have more than one order and do not
say which one you mean, we will ask you to confirm it before we give details.

## Support guidance (internal)
If the customer has more than one open order and does not name one, ask which order they mean and list the
candidates by order number and item. Do not guess. Only discuss orders on the account that matches the sender's
email address (see KB-SEC-01).
