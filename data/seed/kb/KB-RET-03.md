category: returns

---

## Key facts
- Items marked final sale when you buy them cannot be returned or exchanged.
- This applies even when the request is made within 30 days of delivery.

## Details
Final-sale items are marked on the product page and in your order confirmation. We are sorry that we cannot
accept them back.

## Support guidance (internal)
Decline the return under this policy, politely and without offering an exception. Do not refer the customer to
a different policy that does not apply.
